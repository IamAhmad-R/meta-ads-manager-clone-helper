Personalised destinations

Choose which “Personalised destinations” features are turned off when you remove creative enhancements. Meta sends people to the experience most likely to convert after they click your ad. All three are on by default — turn a switch off to leave that feature untouched.

Speed and refresh

Each ad costs 3 Graph calls (read creative, create creative, update ad), plus one read-back when verification is on. Parallel calls shorten long batches, but every mutation counts against Meta's ad-account rate limit.
